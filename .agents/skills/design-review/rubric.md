# Design review rubric

Each sentence is judged from the screenshots alone. Answer yes or no for each page, in light and dark.

1. The page has one clear focal point, and its hierarchy reads at a glance.
2. Elements align to one grid, and none stands alone without a reason.
3. Light and dark are of equal quality: no surface is washed out or unreadable in either.
4. Empty space is deliberate: nothing looks unfinished or cluttered.
5. The page uses stock components only, with no decorative additions.
